Orientation

Orientation: What We Are Building

  • Science-aware & neuro-affirming
    Built on the same foundations as Path of the Dragon and The Crucible of Flesh: nervous system literacy, trauma-informed pacing, and the biology of regulation. We do not bypass the body to get to the spirit.

  • From cosmology to micro-moves
    We take high-concept ideas like the Entangled Firmament, Field–Resonance–Action (FRA), and the Five Energetic Bodies and grind them down into concrete, daily practices you can use in the kitchen, the boardroom, and the quiet.

  • Rooted in ethical, responsible power
    There is no hierarchy of the “enlightened” here. We reject guru-logic. Power is treated as sacred responsibility; boundaries and consent are the floor, not the ceiling.

Who This Is For

  • Sensitive nervous systems — people who process the world at high fidelity and want tools to carry the load without numbing out.
  • Neurodivergent minds — Autism, ADHD, Tourette’s, and other operating systems that chafe against neurotypical spiritual containers.
  • Space holders — therapists, facilitators, and leaders who feel the weight of the Prism of Impact and want clean language for ethics and power.
  • Readers of the book — those who felt the Path of the Dragon in their bones and are ready to create a Sacred Vessel to hold it.

If you are seeking:

  • quick fixes,
  • a new identity to hide in,
  • or a guru to hand your agency to,

this is not your school.

The Architecture (What You’ll Find)

1. Foundations of the Path

A core curriculum in nervous-system regulation, the Somatic Triad, FRA, the Five Energetic Bodies, and the ethics of power.

2. The Practice Library

Short, potent tools for daily life: Daily Bridge check-ins, Doorway Rituals, and Micro-Repairs.

3. The Laboratory

Small cohorts and live labs where we practice slowly, with care, not performance.

4. The Mirror (AI Companion)

Ask the Dragon — an AI-supported reflection tool grounded in the book’s framework. It offers no answers, only better questions to help you explore your own patterns.

Current Status: The Pilot Phase

We are currently in a pilot phase, working with a small number of humans to ensure the nervous system of the School is regulated.

  • We start small, so the container can actually hold what moves through it.
  • We test every practice against biology and ethics, not just aesthetics.
  • We allow the curriculum to emerge from what proves resilient over time.

Safety & Scope

The School of the Dragon is not a replacement for therapy, crisis services, or medical care.

We work with:

  • meaning,
  • frameworks,
  • practices,
  • and community.

If you are in acute crisis, your first step is local professional support and safety.
The Dragon’s Path can walk with you alongside that — not instead of it.
