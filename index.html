The School of the Dragon

Early idea

School of the Dragon is still mostly an idea.

The book exists. The school does not, at least not yet.

What exists is an intention: to build a careful companion to Path of the Dragon grounded in nervous-system literacy, discernment, boundaries, shadow work, and an ethical relationship to power.

This page names that intention without pretending it is already a finished offer.

What This Is

The School is not a course factory, a guru container, or a new spiritual identity. It is a direction for how this work might continue off the page: slower, more embodied, and more accountable than most spiritual offerings.

Practice Before Performance

The emphasis is on exercises, reflection, and lived application, not on collecting concepts or sounding initiated.

Language That Holds Up

Translate symbolic and philosophical material into questions and practices you can actually use when something real is happening.

Slow, Careful Pacing

Move slowly enough that honesty, boundaries, and repair stay real.

Who This Is For

  • Readers of the book who want a place to practice rather than stay in theory.
  • Sensitive or neurodivergent nervous systems that need pacing, clarity, and less performative spirituality.
  • Therapists, facilitators, and leaders who want cleaner language for power, boundaries, discernment, and repair.
  • People who have been burned by bypass or charisma and still want depth that stays accountable to the body and to reality.

This is not for quick fixes, borrowed certainty, or anyone looking for a substitute for therapy, medical care, or crisis support.

What It Points Toward

If this ever becomes real, these are the principles it should have to protect:

  1. Regulation before intensity.
    No glamour around overwhelm. No treating activation as truth. Capacity comes first.

  2. Consent, accountability, and repair before charisma.
    No guru logic. No borrowed authority. No spiritual claim that outranks impact.

  3. Lived consequence before metaphysical claim.
    The test is not whether something sounds deep. The test is whether it changes how a person relates, chooses, and makes amends.

What Happens Now

There is no program to join yet. There is only the work itself, the emerging idea around it, and the question of whether it should become something more concrete.

  1. Read enough of Path of the Dragon to know the tone and framework.
  2. Use the contact form if you want to signal interest, resonance, or skepticism about what this could become.
  3. Let time decide whether this deserves a real form.

That is the honest state of things.

Safety & Scope

The School of the Dragon is educational and practice-based. It is not a replacement for therapy, crisis services, or medical care.

You will not be asked to override your body, perform insight, or force intensity. The standard is simple: move at the speed of trust, stay in contact with reality, and treat power as something that must remain accountable to ethics.

If you are in acute crisis, the first step is qualified local support. This work can sit alongside that, not replace it.

Start With Contact

If this direction resonates, use the contact form on Path of the Dragon and say a little about who you are, how you found the work, and what you hope this could become.

You do not need to be polished or certain. Honest interest is enough.
