The School of the Dragon

Early idea

An aspiration for a future practice space around Path of the Dragon, not yet a formed school.

Right now, the School of the Dragon is mostly an idea: a direction, a hope, and a standard for what this work could become if it earns real form.

The aspiration is to build a careful companion to Path of the Dragon: something grounded in nervous-system literacy, discernment, boundaries, shadow work, and an ethical relationship to power.

Nothing here should be read as a finished offer. It is a public articulation of intent while the actual shape is still unproven.

What This Is

The School is not a course factory, a guru container, or a new spiritual identity. At present, it is not even a live school. It is an attempt to name what might be worth building around Path of the Dragon if it can be built with enough honesty, steadiness, and restraint.

Practice Before Performance

If this ever becomes real, the emphasis should be on exercises, reflection, and lived application, not on collecting concepts or sounding initiated.

Language That Holds Up

The aim would be to translate symbolic and philosophical material into questions and practices you can actually use when something real is happening.

Slow, Careful Pacing

The point is not to launch quickly. The point is to avoid pretending there is a container before there is one.

Who This Is For

  • Readers of the book who would want a place to practice rather than stay in theory.
  • Sensitive or neurodivergent nervous systems that need pacing, clarity, and less performative spirituality.
  • Therapists, facilitators, and leaders who want cleaner language for power, boundaries, discernment, and repair.
  • People who have been burned by bypass or charisma and still want depth that stays accountable to the body and to reality.

This is not for quick fixes, borrowed certainty, or anyone looking for a substitute for therapy, medical care, or crisis support.

What It May Become

If the School earns its way into reality, this is the shape it is pointing toward:

Foundations

Core teachings from the book translated into practical language around regulation, integration, and ethical use of power.

Practice Library

Short exercises, check-ins, and prompts designed to help you notice patterns and make cleaner choices in daily life.

Live Labs

Small-group spaces for guided practice where pacing matters more than intensity and honesty matters more than performance.

Ask the Dragon

An optional AI reflection companion grounded in the book’s framework. It would be for inquiry, not authority.

What Happens Now

There is no program to join yet. There is only the work itself, the emerging idea around it, and the question of whether this should become something more concrete.

  1. Read enough of Path of the Dragon to know the tone and framework.
  2. Use the contact form if you want to signal interest, resonance, or skepticism about what this could become.
  3. If enough substance gathers over time, that feedback may help shape whatever comes next.

The honest answer is that this remains mostly aspirational. The aim is to let reality decide whether it deserves a form, rather than announcing one too early.

Safety & Scope

The School of the Dragon is educational and practice-based. It is not a replacement for therapy, crisis services, or medical care.

You will not be asked to override your body, perform insight, or force intensity. The standard is simple: move at the speed of trust, stay in contact with reality, and treat power as something that must remain accountable to ethics.

If you are in acute crisis, the first step is qualified local support. This work can sit alongside that, not replace it.

Start With Contact

If this direction resonates, use the contact form on Path of the Dragon and say a little about who you are, how you found the work, and what you would hope this might become.

You do not need to be polished or certain. Honest interest is enough.
