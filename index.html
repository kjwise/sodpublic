School of the Dragon

Where Nervous Systems, Power, and Ethics Learn to Move Together

The School of the Dragon is the living companion to Path of the Dragon.

If the book is the map and the Dragon is the archetype,
the School is the practice ground where real humans, with real nervous systems, learn to move differently — in their bodies, relationships, and communities.

What This Is

  • Trauma-aware, neuro-affirming learning
    Built on the same foundations as Path of the Dragon: nervous system literacy, ethical power, and fractal reality — without bypassing biology or complexity.

  • From cosmology to micro-moves
    We take ideas like the Entangled Firmament, Field–Resonance–Action (FRA), and the Five Energetic Bodies and turn them into concrete daily practices.

  • Rooted in ethics, not guru-logic
    No hierarchy of the “enlightened” over the “unawakened.”
    Power is treated as sacred responsibility. Boundaries and consent are non-negotiable.

Who the School Is For

  • People who feel too much, too fast, too deep — and want skill, not shame.
  • Neurodivergent minds (Autism, ADHD, Tourette’s, etc.) who are tired of forcing themselves into neurotypical spiritual containers.
  • Helpers, facilitators, therapists, and leaders who sense they are working inside a bigger web and want clean language for ethics, power, and impact.
  • Readers of Path of the Dragon who feel the book in their bones and want a structured way to live it.

If you are seeking:

  • quick fixes,
  • a new identity to hide in,
  • or a guru to hand your agency to,

this is not your school.

What You’ll Find Here (As We Grow)

  • Foundations of the Dragon’s Path
    A core curriculum in nervous-system literacy, FRA, the Five Energetic Bodies, and ethical power.

  • Practice Libraries
    Short, guided practices: Daily Bridge check-ins, micro-repairs, doorway rituals, Void-safe meditations.

  • Group Containers
    Cohorts, circles, and labs where we practice together — slowly, with care, not performance.

  • Ask the Dragon
    An AI-supported reflection companion, grounded in the book’s framework, to help you explore your own patterns and questions.

This will unfold in iterations. We begin small, test everything against reality and ethics, and let the curriculum emerge from what actually helps.

Safety & Scope

The School of the Dragon is not a replacement for therapy, crisis services, or medical care.

We work with:

  • meaning,
  • frameworks,
  • practices,
  • and community.

If you are in acute crisis, your first step is local professional support and safety.
The Dragon’s Path can walk with you alongside that — not instead of it.
